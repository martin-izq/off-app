Life happens off.

Off is a game you win by putting your phone down. No blockers, no lectures. You earn points for the time your phone stays locked, and you play it best with friends.

HOW YOU EARN
• 1 point for every 30 minutes with your phone locked, from 8 am to midnight
• A bonus for every 2 hours straight without unlocking
• Steps only count while your phone is locked: walking while scrolling pays nothing
• Cycling, gym, swimming and other workouts earn points too
• Fewer unlocks than your average? Extra points at the end of the day
• Start a session of 30 min, 1 h or 2 h: hold out until the end and the blocks count double

AND HOW YOU LOSE THEM
Up to your usual social media time, nothing happens. Go over it and every 5 extra minutes costs you a point.

FIRST TO UNLOCK PAYS
Start a group with friends, your partner or your study crew. When someone goes over on social media, those points go into the group pot. The pot is only split when you meet in person: you scan each other's QR codes at the table and every phone works out the same result.
At a hangout, every 30 minutes with every phone locked counts double. The first one to unlock puts 5 points into the pot.

KEEP IT GOING
• A daily goal, a streak that multiplies your bonus and one free pass a week
• Levels that never go down
• A weekly summary every Sunday
• Home screen widgets
• Share your week as a story, in four designs

WHAT HAPPENS OFF, STAYS OFF
No account. No servers. No ads. Your points, your usage and your groups stay on your phone, and groups sync face to face.

PERMISSIONS
• Usage access (required): to see when your phone is locked and how long you spend on social media. Nothing leaves your phone.
• Health Connect (optional): to read your steps and workouts. We never write to Health Connect, and workouts entered by hand don't count.
• Notifications (optional): when you hit your goal, finish a session or get your Sunday summary.

Off is free. Donations, once they open, never unlock features.
